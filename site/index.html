Architecture of the Agentive World

The agentic world coexists with applications; the agentive one collapses them. Only the latter makes the real-time enterprise possible — the one that stopped using people as infrastructure. This corpus maps the whole transition: the destination, the route, and the architecture that makes it operable —and governable— in production.
