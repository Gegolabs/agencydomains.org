Chapter 3 · The Seven Mechanisms

Six operating mechanisms and one deliberate exception

Figure F3
1 · Intelligent routing

It routes, it doesn't publish

Who needs to know, in what detail, with what urgency. Selective silence is half the value.

2 · Adaptive formatting

Reformulates per receiver

Impact for the executive, deadlines for the manager, instructions for the technician.

7 · The agora

The deliberate exception

The cafeteria, the hallway, the open space: direct conversation, unmediated, with no declared purpose. The human side of conversation lives here.

The agent doesn't listen unless explicitly invoked.
The agora doesn't feed the agent unless a human asks.
In corporate chat everything is agora; in Postchat the agora is a place, not a flow.
3 · Proactive briefing

Interruptions, grouped

Requires your action · worth knowing · resolved without you — and the noise, absorbed in silence.

4 · Orchestrated decision

The process replaces the room

Framing → individual consultation → synthesis → second round → final package. Anchoring disappears.

5 · Collective memory

Graph, not history

Decisions, commitments, precedents — with context and sources, not twenty search results.

6 · Closed loop

Nothing goes unanswered

The system pursues closure; people stop spending themselves on the chase.

The principle that governs them: the attention budget
the metric: hours of uninterrupted work
protected per person per day
