Chapter 2 · Communication Comes to You

Two species of communication infrastructure

Figure F2

Interruption factory

It exposes you to a flow and leaves the filtering work to you. It generates interruptions.
flow ───────▶ YOU (filter) noise ──────▶ YOU (filter) urgent ─────▶ YOU (filter) joke ───────▶ YOU (filter)
Natural metric: engagement
how many times it pulls you back in
The corporate channel is a factory by design; an AI copilot inside the channel is the same factory with help.

Interruption absorber

It intercepts the flow and delivers only what justifies attention. It consumes interruptions.
flow ───▶ ┐ noise ──▶ │ AGENT ──▶ briefing urgent ─▶ │ (absorbs) (+ real urgency) joke ───▶ ┘ ──▶ YOU (decide)
Natural metric: hours of uninterrupted work protected
how many hours it managed to keep anyone from touching you
Postchat belongs to this species: silence as a guarantee, not a threat.
They are not degrees of the same thing: they are opposite directions.
