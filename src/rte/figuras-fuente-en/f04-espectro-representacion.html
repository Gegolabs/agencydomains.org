Chapter 5 · The Agent That Speaks for You

The maturity spectrum of representation

Figure F4
Stage 1

Assistant

Helps within the task: drafts, summarizes, searches.
Acts: the person, on every interaction
Stage 2

Mediator

Takes on the full communicational logistics (chs. 2–4).
The person decides; the agent transports
Stage 3

Representative in consultation

Answers for its user: positions, conditions, criteria — and flags what can't be delegated.
The agent informs; the final word is the person's
Stage 4

Representative with delegation

Decides and executes within explicit parameters: approves the minor, schedules the routine.
The agent, within the framework; the person reviews and can revert
accumulated context + trust granted → The order can't be skipped: representation is cultivated through months of mediation.
