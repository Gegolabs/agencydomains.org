## The Trilogy

*The Agentive World Trilogy, in five parts (for now).*

The transition that occupies the trilogy is a single one: from the world where people open applications to work, to the world where AI agents are the interface of work. It is four books and a reference implementation. Each component answers one question of that transition, and each one reads complete on its own.

The four books were written in the reverse order of reading, like a stack: *AgencyDomains* came first and ended up at the bottom; this book, the last, ended up on top. That is why a book can cite another that is read after it: it cites it because it had already been written.

**I · The Death of Software as We Know It** *(this book)*
Why now? **The starting point.** Why describing a business once and freezing it in a piece of software, whether rented, licensed or custom-built, stops being the way to automate it; what is lost when every organization builds its own; and which piece survives the change: the system of truth.

**II · The Real-Time Enterprise · The Agentive World**
Where are we going? **The destination.**

**III · AURA · The Agentive Path**
Which way? **The route.**

**IV · AgencyDomains · Agentive Architecture**
How do we get there? **The vehicle.**

**A · Vergis · Agentive Technology** *(the reference implementation: not a book, it's code)*
What do I start with today? **The keys.**
