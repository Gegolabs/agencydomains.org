From the classic cycle to the continuous intelligence cycle

Figure G5

Classic cycle — linear and closed

"people go to the data"

1Descriptive — what happened
2Diagnostic — why it happened
3Predictive — what will happen
4Prescriptive — what to do
5Human evaluates and decides ← open loop
The cycle's speed is tied to the human's speed. If they sleep, the cycle does not advance.

Agentive cycle — continuous and closed

"intelligence goes to the people and acts on their behalf"

monitor
interpret
decide + execute
learn
Human above the loop — sets thresholds, governs, handles exceptions
The system closes the loop. The organization stops doing analytics to be an intelligent system.
Online enterprise (human closes the loop) → Real-time enterprise (agent closes the loop, human governs)
