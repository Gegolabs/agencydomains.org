Internal · MCP MCP carries the internal Layer 2 → Layer 3 interface: the Cognition commands its muscle memory (the Botler and its Botlets).
Between agents · A2A A2A is the relation between AgencyDomains — distinct agents. It describes nothing that happens within a single domain.
Coordination between Botlers of the same AgencyDomain goes "via the A2A protocol" — it is not "internal A2A".
MCP is the internal Cognition→Botler interface; A2A is the relation between AgencyDomains.AgencyDomains · v0.4
