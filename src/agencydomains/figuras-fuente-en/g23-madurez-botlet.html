The agentic fallback guarantee is what produces maturity
Each Botlet failure due to an environment change activates the cognition; each activation regenerates the Botlet incorporating the variant. Without agentic fallback, the Botlet would stay trapped in its initial version — it would not mature.
Reliable offline is a senior property Expecting a freshly deployed Botlet to operate offline violates the spec — it is still junior, it depends on cognition.
Operational continuity ≠ fallback Continuity operates over senior Botlets down by exogenous cause. Fallback operates over any phase with cognition available.
Maturity is traceable The append-only log records invocations, failures, and regenerations. The junior → senior trajectory is auditable, not an assumption.
