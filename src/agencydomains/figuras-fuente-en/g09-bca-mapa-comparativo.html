How to read the map

X axis (measurable). Five levels of how much logic the component handling authoritative data accumulates. Verifiable criteria.
Y axis (qualitative). How dogmatic the proposal is about demanding its separation, vs how much gray it admits. Interpretive inference formalized in the next section.
Purists (top). CQRS, Helland, Clean — "if you don't separate strictly, you're not really doing X".
Pragmatists (bottom). Service Layer, Active Record, 3-layer — Fowler himself says "the line gets muddied in practice".
BCA occupies the middle ground: it adopts the formality of Moore and Fowler at the domain boundary, but accepts COTS legacy systems and agents being onboarded.
The Y axis reflects what Fowler himself acknowledges as "the gray": between perfect theory and practice that gets muddied. Pure DAO sits in neutral pragmatism because it is not an architectural doctrine — it is just a mechanism.
