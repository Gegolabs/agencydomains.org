Online enterprise → real-time enterprise · the Quantum Leap

Figure G6
The Quantum Leap

Online enterprise

data up to date — but the human closes the loop

Datacurrent, dashboards to the second
Decisionsmade by a human looking at the report
Analytical capacitylimited by a pre-built menu
Answer to a new question4 to 12 weeks (a BI project)
Bottleneckhuman attention
Valuable skilloperating dashboards

Real-time enterprise

detects · interprets · decides · acts — continuously

Datasemantic layer actionable by agents
Decisionsmade by the agent; the human governs
Analytical capacityelastic — any question over available data
Answer to a new question5 to 60 seconds (a conversation)
Bottleneckanything but human attention
Valuable skilldirecting agents
It is not a faster version of the previous model — it is something else. The difference is not one of speed, it is one of operating model.
