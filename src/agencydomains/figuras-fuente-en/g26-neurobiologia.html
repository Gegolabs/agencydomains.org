Pattern Recognition · Ch. 4 §2
Level 1 · milliseconds

Fast familiarity

have I seen this before?
Perirhinal cortex familiarity without conscious recollection
Pattern detection recent activity trace vs. past invocations
If the answer is yes, the next level activates.
Level 2 · seconds

Recollection

what exactly did I do last time?
Hippocampus detailed retrieval of past events
Trace retrieval parameters, results, durations of similar invocations
The cognition uses this information to assess whether generation is worth it.
Level 3 · reasoning

Conscious decision

should I automate this?
Prefrontal cortex deliberate, executive evaluation
Evaluation + generation frequency · stability · cost · regeneration risk
If the decision is yes, it triggers Botlet generation.
