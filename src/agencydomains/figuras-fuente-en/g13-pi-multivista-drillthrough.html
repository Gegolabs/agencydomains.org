The context K bounds within what the viewer can already see (the destination's RLS): it narrows, never widens — intersection, never union. Drill-through is not a back door to unauthorized data.

authz-blind

Views and drill-through edges declare no authorization. Who sees what is decided by the data's RLS at the destination, not by the navigation graph.
