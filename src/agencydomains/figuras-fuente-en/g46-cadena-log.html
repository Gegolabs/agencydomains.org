Each block contains the hash of the previous one. A retroactive alteration breaks the chain across every subsequent hash — and is detected immediately.
Immutability Records are never edited or deleted — only appended.
Cryptographic chaining Hash of the previous record in each new record. Verifiable chain.
Hash of parameters, not content The log stores no sensitive data — their hashes. Raw data in a separate store.
Retention per domain Financial ~7 years · health ~10 · whatever regulation demands.
✗ Application log (does not suffice) Sometimes editable · no chaining · retention days/weeks · audience: developers.
✓ Trust Infrastructure append-only log Immutable · chained · retention in years · audience: auditors and regulators.
A serious organization keeps both — and does not confuse themAgentive Architecture · v1.2
