Three typical triggers

By explicit policy The agent's policy declares require_approval: true for the operation.
By threshold Exceeds a configured threshold (amount, scope, severity). E.g. transfer > $100k.
By agent uncertainty The agent declares low confidence in its decision and requests validation.

Four operating rules

Authenticated approver — verified identity, no anonymous click
Configurable timeout — once expired, abort (safe default)
Immutability — the decision lands in the append-only log
Batch approval — only with discipline, recorded and time-bounded
