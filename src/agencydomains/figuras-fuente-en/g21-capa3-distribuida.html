Offline mode emerges as a structural property. When the edge Botlets are senior, the physical site operates against the local DB + edge-resident Capabilities without network. The queue toward central accumulates; when the network returns, it drains.
It is not federation. The Botlers cooperate within the same AgencyDomain with a single identity, single log, and uniform Trust regime. Intra-AgencyDomain coordination operates between Botlers via the A2A protocol; federation —the A2A relation between AgencyDomains— operates between distinct AgencyDomains.
